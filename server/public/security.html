Security

Last updated: 2026-11-16

How Vouch protects your account and data.

1. Authentication

Production sign-in uses Google Sign-In. We never store passwords. Access tokens expire after 30 days and are stored in the device secure store.

2. Transport and storage

All production traffic is served over HTTPS. Data is stored in a managed Postgres database with restricted access. Backups are taken daily by the hosting provider.

3. Payments

Payments are disabled during the beta. No card or bank data is collected by Vouch.

4. Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security issue, email thedesaimitul@gmail.com with the details and steps to reproduce. Please do not publicly disclose the issue before we have had a chance to fix it. We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 working days.

5. What we ask of you

  • Keep your device and Google account secure.
  • Report suspicious activity or impersonation.
  • Do not attempt to access other users' accounts or data.
