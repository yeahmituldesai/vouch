Security

Last updated: 2026-11-15

How Vouch protects your account and data.

1. Authentication

Sign-in uses one-time codes sent to your phone, or Google Sign-In. We never store passwords. Access tokens expire after 30 days and are stored in the device secure store.

2. Transport and storage

All production traffic is served over HTTPS. Data is stored in a managed Postgres database with restricted access. Backups are taken daily by the hosting provider.

3. Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security issue, email security@vouch.example.com with details. Please do not disclose publicly until we have had a chance to fix it. We acknowledge reports within 3 working days.

4. Account safety tips

  • Never share your sign-in code with anyone.
  • Sign out on shared devices.
  • Delete your account (Settings → Delete account) if you no longer use Vouch.
  • Report abusive or unsafe content using the Report option in the app.

5. Moderation

We remove illegal, abusive or unsafe content and may suspend accounts that break our terms. Report problems from inside the app or by email.
