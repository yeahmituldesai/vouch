Support

Last updated: 2026-11-15

Need help with Vouch? We are here for you.

1. Contact

Email support@vouch.example.com. We reply within 2 working days.

2. In-app help

  • Report an idea or user: open the idea, tap the menu (⋯), choose Report.
  • Block a user: open their profile or idea, tap the menu, choose Block.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account.
  • Edit your profile: Profile → Edit profile.

3. Common questions

  • How do I post an idea? Tap the + button in the tab bar, fill in the details, and submit.
  • What is vouching? Vouching shows support for an idea; it is not a payment.
  • What does "I'm interested" mean? During the beta it records demand; no money moves.
  • How do refunds work? See the Refund Policy. During the beta there is nothing to refund.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, vouches, comments and follows.

4. Safety

To report abusive content, use the Report option inside the app or email us. For security issues, email security@vouch.example.com.
