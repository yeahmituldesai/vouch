Support

Last updated: 2026-11-16

Need help with Vouch? We are here for you.

1. Contact

Email thedesaimitul@gmail.com. We reply within 2 working days.

2. In-app help

  • Report an idea or user: open the idea, tap the menu (⋯), choose Report.
  • Block a user: open their profile, tap ⋯, choose Block.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account.
  • Edit your profile: Profile → Edit profile.

3. Common questions

  • How do I sign in? Vouch uses Google Sign-In. Tap "Continue with Google" on the welcome screen.
  • How do I post an idea? Tap the + button in the tab bar, fill in the name, category, price, goal and deadline, then publish.
  • What does "vouch" mean? A vouch is a public signal that you would consider buying the product. It helps founders judge demand before they build.
  • What does "I'm interested" mean? During the beta it records demand without any payment. No money moves.
  • Why did an idea disappear? Ideas that miss their goal by the deadline expire, and ideas that break the rules can be removed by moderation.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, ideas, vouches and comments.

4. Beta limitations

Vouch is in beta. Payments are disabled, image uploads may be unavailable, and features can change without notice. Please report anything that looks broken.
