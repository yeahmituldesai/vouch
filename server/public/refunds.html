Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

1. Beta period

Vouch is currently in beta. No payments are taken: people tap "I'm interested" instead of paying, so there is nothing to refund during the beta.

2. When payments are enabled

  • Pre-orders are refunded automatically if an idea misses its goal by the deadline.
  • If an idea is removed by moderation, paid orders are refunded.
  • Founders receive funds only after the goal is met.

3. How refunds are processed

Refunds are issued to the original payment method through our payment provider. Processing time depends on the provider and your bank (typically 5–10 working days).

4. Disputes

If you believe a refund is missing or incorrect, email thedesaimitul@gmail.com with your order details and we will investigate.
