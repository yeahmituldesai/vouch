Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

Vouch runs the Vouch app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

1. What we collect

  • Account: Google account ID, email and name, plus the handle, bio, avatar and banner you add. Google Sign-In is the only production sign-in method.
  • Content: ideas, comments, vouches, "I'm interested" taps, follows and reports.
  • Device and log data: device type, OS, app version, IP address, timestamps and a push-notification token.
  • Payment data (only if payments are ever enabled): handled by a payment provider; we store order IDs and amounts, never card numbers. Payments are disabled during the beta.

2. How we use it

To run your account, show your profile and ideas to others, sign you in, notify you of activity, moderate the service, and improve the app.

3. Who we share it with

  • Google Sign-In, to verify your identity when you sign in.
  • Hosting provider (Render) and database provider (Supabase/Postgres) to run the service.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging, to deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Other users: your name, handle, avatar, bio, and the ideas, vouches and comments you post are public.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in a managed Postgres database. We never store passwords; sign-in uses Google Sign-In. Access tokens expire after 30 days. All traffic is served over HTTPS in production.

5. Your rights

You can edit your profile, delete your account (Settings → Delete account), and ask us to remove your data by emailing thedesaimitul@gmail.com. We reply within the time required by law.

6. Children

Vouch is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will notify you in the app. Continued use after an update means you accept the new policy.
