Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

Vouch runs the Vouch app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

1. What we collect

  • Account: phone number or Google account ID and email, name, handle, bio, avatar and banner images.
  • Content: ideas, comments, vouches, "I'm interested" taps, follows, reports.
  • Device and log data: device type, OS, app version, IP address, timestamps, push token.
  • Payment data (only when payments are enabled): handled by our payment provider; we store order IDs and amounts, never card numbers.

2. How we use it

To run your account, show your profile and ideas to others, send sign-in codes, notify you of activity, moderate the service, and improve the app.

3. Who we share it with

  • SMS provider (MSG91, or console in development) to send sign-in codes.
  • Hosting provider (Render) and database provider (Supabase/Postgres) to run the service.
  • Payment provider (Razorpay) when payments are enabled.
  • Other users: your name, handle, avatar, bio, and the ideas, vouches and comments you post are public.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in a managed Postgres database. We never store passwords; sign-in uses one-time codes. Access tokens expire after 30 days. All traffic is served over HTTPS in production.

5. Your rights

You can edit your profile, delete your account (Settings → Delete account), and ask us to remove your data by emailing support@vouch.example.com. We reply within the time required by law.

6. Children

Vouch is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and will notify you in the app. Continued use after an update means you accept the new policy.

8. Grievance officer (India)

Vouch Team, support@vouch.example.com, +91 99999 99999. We reply within the time required by law.
