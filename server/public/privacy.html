Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

Vouch runs the Vouch app. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

Your phone number or Google account, name, handle, bio, and the ideas, vouches, comments and pre-orders you create. We also collect basic device and log data to keep the service running.

How we use it

To run your account, show your ideas and profile to others, send sign-in codes, notify you of activity on your ideas, and moderate the service.

Who we share it with

Our SMS provider (MSG91 or console in development) to send sign-in codes, our hosting provider (Render), and our database provider (Supabase). Founders see your name and handle when you vouch, comment or pre-order. We do not sell your data.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a managed Postgres database. Passwords are never stored; sign-in uses one-time codes. Access tokens expire after 30 days.

Your rights

You can edit your profile, delete your account (Settings → Delete account), and ask us to remove your data by emailing support@vouch.example.com.

Grievance officer

Vouch Team, support@vouch.example.com, +91 99999 99999. We reply within the time required by law.
